## Source limitations

Direct Reddit tooling was unavailable in the local research environment, so Reddit evidence was limited to public search results and was never treated as authoritative. No community repository was installed or executed. Publication dates were unavailable for several living documentation pages; those are marked “live” rather than assigned a fabricated date.
