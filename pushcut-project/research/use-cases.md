## Selection rationale

The top ten maximize value without requiring a permanently foreground iOS server. Pushcut is strongest as the fast human-facing edge of a server-owned workflow. Direct repair, emergency stop, and mobile identity score high in value but also in security risk and implementation difficulty; they are later phases, not the first live test.
